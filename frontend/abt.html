Who We Are

A Training Institute
Built for Real Careers

DATA SPARK TECHNOLOGIES was founded with one clear mission — to bridge the gap between classroom learning and real industry demands. We saw that students needed more than just theory; they needed hands-on skills they could use from day one on the job.

Based in Chennai, we specialize in Web Development, Python, Software Testing, and Data Analytics — four of the most in-demand skills in the IT sector today. Every course is crafted by industry experts and delivered with a focus on practical, project-based learning.

With a strong track record of successful placements and hundreds of trained students, we continue to grow as one of Chennai's most trusted tech training institutes.

How We Got Here

A timeline of growth, milestones, and student success

Year 1 — Foundation
